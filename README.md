Payer provides a modern B2B payments platform that automates order-to-cash processes, offering flexible payment methods, invoicing, and reconciliation. Their solution helps financial, commercial, and technical leaders accelerate cash flow, reduce manual effort, and enable new business models. The platform includes APIs for payment initiation, invoice management, and reporting, aiming to streamline payments for enterprises.

**Areas:** Company · Payments · B2B · Fintech · Automation

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Payer: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
